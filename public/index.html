PRODUCTION, NOT PROMISES

Your deploy isn't done when CI turns green.
It's done when production proves it.

ProofShip inspects the release, tests real production behavior, diagnoses regressions, coordinates GitHub, Vercel, Linear and Slack, and keeps the evidence for every decision.

The included scenarios are deterministic. External actions stay simulated unless live demo integrations are explicitly enabled.
